# Station screen surface

Mode: Operate. This extends the established Sime Loop app identity; the user specifically asked for a small vending-machine screen with two choices, QR pairing and a local leaderboard. No alternate visual world or concept selection is needed for that specified layout.

THESIS: A resident can choose a waste stream, claim one deposit with a phone, and understand when it is safe to sort.

OWN-WORLD: Existing DM Sans typography, brand red #D71920, charcoal #292929, neutral #F5F5F5 background, green recyclable and pale-orange food surfaces. Reuse the actual app brand and controls. Authored vector material illustrations carry the two choices.

STORY: Choose category → scan expiring QR → place visible items on plate → request camera check → resolve mismatch or wait for sorting → see points. Category selection reserves a destination; its internal door stays closed until verification.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two large touch targets lead. The station name and pairing settings sit above; the community leaderboard and all three capacities sit alongside on iPad/desktop and below on phones. Phone preview retains both choices through vertical scrolling.

FORM: Precisely specified two-choice kiosk in the existing product system. Touch targets at least 44 px, readable status labels, a 220 px QR plus quiet zone, explicit demo controls, no unsupported savings claims. Landscape iPad gets compact spacing; mobile reflows to a single column.

Required evidence: kiosk desktop, 1024×768 iPad and 390×844 phone; a QR state; mismatch and success actions; resident claim link through sign-in. No raster comp was requested or generated. The optional Impeccable engine/detector is unavailable in this environment; the references and manual review are used.

REVIEW: The final landing screenshots in `.impeccable/review/kiosk-desktop.png`, `kiosk-tablet.png` and `kiosk-mobile.png` received a ship verdict with no material visual fixes. The pale-green recyclable and pale-orange food choices lead beneath the two-line headline; the local leaderboard and three capacity bars remain secondary. Both choices and the sidebar fit the landscape iPad; the phone stacks the choices and uses vertical scrolling. This verdict covers the captured landing layouts, not physical camera or sensor validation.
